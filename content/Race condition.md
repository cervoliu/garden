> A **race condition** or **race hazard** is the condition of an [electronics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronics "Electronics"), [software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software "Software"), or other [system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System) where the system's substantive behavior is [dependent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequential_logic "Sequential logic") on the sequence or timing of other uncontrollable events, leading to unexpected or inconsistent results. It becomes a [bug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_bug "Software bug") when one or more of the possible behaviors is undesirable.

竞态条件是电路，软件等系统中一种不希望发生的现象：系统实质行为依赖于其他不可控事件的顺序或时间，从而导致预期外或不一致的结果。
