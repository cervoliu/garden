a.k.a. SAT, the most famous NP-Complete Problem.

The proof of SAT to be NP-Complete is given by [Cook-Levin Theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cook%E2%80%93Levin_theorem).

The decision-procedures for SAT are [DPLL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DPLL_algorithm) and [CDCL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conflict-driven_clause_learning).
