#tensor #outer-product

张量外积是一种扩张维度的二元运算，应该与笛卡尔积是同义词

> Wikipedia: Given two tensors $\mathbf {u} ,\mathbf {v}$ with dimensions $(k_{1},k_{2},\dots ,k_{m})$ and $(l_1,l_2,\dots,l_n)$, their outer product $\mathbf{u} \otimes \mathbf{v}$ is a tensor with dimensions $(k_1,k_2,\dots,k_m,l_1,l_2,\dots,l_n)$ and entries: 
> $$ (\mathbf{u} \otimes \mathbf{v})_{i_1, i_2, \dots, i_m, j_1,j_2,\dots, j_n} = u_{i_1,i_2,\dots,i_m}v_{j_1,j_2,\dots,j_n}$$ 

有时候也用 $\times$ 表示。
